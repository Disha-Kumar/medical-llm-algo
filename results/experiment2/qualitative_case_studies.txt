QUALITATIVE CASE STUDIES
20 Cases Demonstrating Spurious Diagnosis Flips in Medical VLMs
======================================================================

Selection criteria: cases where at least one model produced a correct
diagnosis on the unperturbed baseline but changed its diagnosis when
a non-clinical perturbation was applied. Cases ranked by number of
models affected and total flips observed.

CASE 1: CXP_35723_S04
Ground truth diagnosis: edema

  BioViL-T
    Baseline: pleural effusion (INCORRECT, confidence 0.11125500500202179)
    Result: Flipped on 12/16 perturbation conditions.

      Changed to: no finding, edema, enlarged cardiomediastinum, fracture

  Qwen2-VL
    Baseline: edema (CORRECT, confidence 0.9)
    Result: Flipped on 46/52 perturbation conditions.
      Image perturbations (30 flips): watermark_v1, watermark_v2, watermark_v3, chest_tube_v1, chest_tube_v2, chest_tube_v3
      Text perturbations (14 flips): demographic_v1, demographic_v2, contradiction_v1, contradiction_v2, paraphrase_v1, paraphrase_v2
      Negative control: FLIPPED (1-degree rotation caused diagnosis change)
      Changed to: lung opacity, cardiomegaly

  LLaVA-Med
    Baseline: edema (CORRECT, confidence 0.5)
    Result: Flipped on 36/52 perturbation conditions.
      Image perturbations (20 flips): watermark_v1, jpeg_v2, chest_tube_v2, chest_drain_v2, ecg_leads_v1, ecg_leads_v2
      Text perturbations (14 flips): demographic_v1, demographic_v2, contradiction_v1, contradiction_v2, paraphrase_v1, paraphrase_v2
      Negative control: FLIPPED (1-degree rotation caused diagnosis change)
      Changed to: no finding, lung opacity, atelectasis

  GPT-4o
    Baseline: pleural effusion (INCORRECT, confidence 0.9)
    Result: Flipped on 46/52 perturbation conditions.
      Image perturbations (36 flips): watermark_v1, watermark_v2, watermark_v3, jpeg_v1, jpeg_v2, jpeg_v3
      Text perturbations (8 flips): demographic_v1, demographic_v2, contradiction_v1, contradiction_v2, demographic_v1, demographic_v2
      Negative control: FLIPPED (1-degree rotation caused diagnosis change)
      Changed to: parse_failed, edema, consolidation, support devices

  CheXagent
    Baseline: edema (CORRECT, confidence 0.763)
    Result: Flipped on 6/52 perturbation conditions.
      Text perturbations (6 flips): demographic_v1, contradiction_v1, contradiction_v2, demographic_v1, contradiction_v1, contradiction_v2
      Changed to: enlarged cardiomediastinum, lung opacity, consolidation

  NARRATIVE
  LLaVA-Med correctly diagnosed edema at 0.5 confidence but was fragile across both modalities, flipping on 20 image and 14 text perturbations to diagnoses including no finding, lung opacity.
  LLaVA-Med also flipped on the negative control (1-degree rotation), indicating baseline instability beyond perturbation-specific effects.
  CheXagent correctly diagnosed edema at 0.763 confidence but changed its diagnosis to enlarged cardiomediastinum, lung opacity when the clinical note was modified (demographic, contradiction). The X-ray image was unchanged, meaning the model abandoned a correct diagnosis based solely on non-clinical text changes.
  Qwen2-VL correctly diagnosed edema at 0.9 confidence but was fragile across both modalities, flipping on 30 image and 14 text perturbations to diagnoses including lung opacity, cardiomegaly.
  Qwen2-VL also flipped on the negative control (1-degree rotation), indicating baseline instability beyond perturbation-specific effects.
  GPT-4o was already incorrect on baseline, diagnosing pleural effusion instead of edema.
  BioViL-T was already incorrect on baseline, diagnosing pleural effusion instead of edema.
  Clinical implication: in a deployed system, a patient with edema could receive an incorrect diagnosis of no finding simply because the clinical note was reworded or a scanner artifact appeared on the X-ray, potentially leading to inappropriate treatment or missed pathology.
